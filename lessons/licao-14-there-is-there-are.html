5

📘 Gramática — There is / There are

Estrutura e uso no dia a dia.

Singular

There is + substantivo singular

There is a cat.

Plural

There are + plural

There are three dogs.

Pergunta/Negativa

Is there…? / There isn't…

Are there any chairs?
