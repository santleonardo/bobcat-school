📘 Gramática — Narrar o Passado

Estrutura e uso no dia a dia.

Mistura natural

was/were + past simple

I was tired. I went home.

Perguntas

Did + base / Wh- + did

When did you arrive?

Marcadores

yesterday, last week, ago

three years ago
