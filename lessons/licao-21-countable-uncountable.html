📘 Gramática — Countable vs Uncountable

Estrutura e uso no dia a dia.

Countable

têm plural + a/an + many

an apple / many apples

Uncountable

sem plural; much / some

water, rice, money

some / any

some em +; any em ? e −

I have some tea. Do you have any?
