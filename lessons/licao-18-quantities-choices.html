📘 Gramática — Quantificadores

Estrutura e uso no dia a dia.

a few / a little

few = contável; little = incontável

a few eggs / a little oil

too much / too many

excesso

too much salt / too many cars

enough

após adj. ou antes de substantivo

big enough / enough food
