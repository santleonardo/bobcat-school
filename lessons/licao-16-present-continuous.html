5

📘 Gramática — Present Continuous

Estrutura e uso no dia a dia.

Forma

am/is/are + verbo-ing

She is running.

Uso

ação no momento / temporária

I am living here this month.

vs Simple Present

hábito = present; agora = continuous

I work / I am working now.
