1

🎯 Objetivos

am, is, are — afirmativas, negativas e perguntas

📘

Formas

Usar am / is / are com cada pronome.

➖

Negativas

I'm not, isn't, aren't.

❓

Perguntas

Are you…? Is she…? Yes/No.

💡

Em português há ser e estar. Em inglês os dois viram to be: "Eu sou feliz" e "Eu estou feliz" → I am happy.

➖ Negativas & ❓ Perguntas

Negativas

I am not / I'm not a doctor.
She is not / isn't tired.
They are not / aren't here.

Perguntas Yes/No

Are you a student? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Is she from Brazil? — Yes, she is.
Are they happy? — No, they aren't.
