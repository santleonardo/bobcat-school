5

📘 Gramática — Do/Does e To/For

Estrutura e uso no dia a dia.

Do / Does

Do + I/you/we/they; Does + he/she/it

Does she live here?

To

Direção (to school) ou propósito (to learn)

I go to work to earn money.

For

Benefício (for you) ou duração (for 2 hours)

This gift is for you.
