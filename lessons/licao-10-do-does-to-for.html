📘 Gramática

Do / Does

Do you like coffee?
Does she work here?
I do not (don't) play. · He does not (doesn't) play.

Does = he / she / it · Do = I / you / we / they

To vs For

I go to school. (direção)
This gift is for you. (benefício)
I study to learn. (objetivo/infinitivo)
I waited for 10 minutes. (duração)
