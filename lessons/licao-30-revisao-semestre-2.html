📘 Gramática — Revisão Integrada A2

Estrutura e uso no dia a dia.

Passado e futuro

did / was + going to / will

I went. I'm going to study. I will try.

Quantidade e espaço

there is, much/many, preposições

There are many books on the desk.

Modais e phrasals

should, must, get up, look for

You should get up earlier.
