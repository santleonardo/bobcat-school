LIÇÃO 31 — Past Continuous

Nível A2
🎯 Objetivo da Lição

• Aprender a formar e usar o Past Continuous (was/were + verbo-ing).

• Descrever ações em progresso em um momento específico do passado.

• Usar when e while para conectar ações e contar histórias.

🔥 Warm-Up: O que estava acontecendo?

Pense em ontem às 20h. O que você estava fazendo? Em inglês, para descrever uma ação "em andamento" no passado, usamos o Past Continuous. Compare:

Simple Past: I watched a movie. (ação completa)

Past Continuous: I was watching a movie. (ação em progresso, sem foco no início/fim)

💡 Dica: Past Continuous = was/were + verbo-ing. É o "estava fazendo" em português!

🗣️ Diálogo: Last Night

Lucas: What were you doing at 9 p.m. last night? I called you three times!

Ana: Sorry! I was taking a shower when you called the first time.

Lucas: And the second time?

Ana: I was cooking dinner. I couldn't hear the phone.

Lucas: What about 9:30? I called again.

Ana: Oh, I was sleeping already! I was really tired.

Lucas: Ha! While you were sleeping, I was studying for my English test.

Ana: Good luck with that! What were the other students doing?

Lucas: Some were reviewing grammar, others were chatting in the group.

🔁 Repita com atenção: Leia o diálogo em voz alta. Sublinhe todos os verbos no Past Continuous!

📘 Gramática em Ação: Past Continuous
1. Formação

Afirmativa: subject + was/were + verb-ing

I/He/She/It was working. | You/We/They were working.

Negativa: subject + was/were + not + verb-ing

She wasn't sleeping. | They weren't listening.

Interrogativa: Was/Were + subject + verb-ing?

Were you studying? Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.

2. Regras de ortografia do -ing
RegraExemplo
Verbo termina em -e muda: tira o -emake → making
Verbo curto CVC (consoante-vogal-consoante): dobra a última letrarun → running, sit → sitting
Regra geral: apenas adiciona -ingstudy → studying, cook → cooking
3. Quando usar

Usamos o Past Continuous para:

a) Ação em progresso em um momento específico: At 9 p.m., I was watching TV.

b) Duas ações acontecendo ao mesmo tempo (with while): While I was cooking, she was cleaning.

c) Uma ação interrompida por outra (with when): I was sleeping when you called.

Dica prática: A ação MAIS LONGA (em progresso) usa Past Continuous. A ação CURTA que interrompe usa Simple Past. "I was sleeping (longa) when the phone rang (curta)."
4. When vs While

When geralmente introduz a ação curta (Simple Past). While geralmente introduz a ação longa (Past Continuous).

While I was walking home, it started to rain.

I was walking home when it started to rain.

📝 Exercícios
A. Complete com o Past Continuous do verbo entre parênteses.
I (sleep) at midnight.
They (watch) a movie.
She (cook) dinner.
We (study) English.
It (rain) all afternoon.
B. Escolha a opção correta.
I ___ TV when you called.
While she ___ dinner, the phone rang.
___ you sleeping at 10 p.m.?
They weren't ___ when I arrived.
C. Combine as ações usando when/while.
1. (I / drive / see an accident) →
2. (they / talk / I / arrive) →
3. (while / study / mother / call) →
D. Verdadeiro ou Falso?
"Run" no -ing fica "runing".
"While" geralmente introduz a ação mais longa.
Past Continuous usa "did" na pergunta.
"She was sleeping" significa "ela estava dormindo".
🎭 Prática Oral / Role-Play

A: What were you doing at 8 p.m. yesterday?

B: I was having dinner with my family. What about you?

A: I was working. I was writing an important e-mail when my computer crashed!

B: Oh no! What did you do?

A: I called my colleague while I was trying to fix it.

👅 Trava-língua

While Wendy was walking, William was watching Wendy walking.

🌍 Curiosidade Cultural: Storytelling in English

O Past Continuous é essencial para contar histórias em inglês! Filmes, livros e até piadas frequentemente começam com uma cena em progresso: "Once upon a time, a girl was walking through the forest..." Isso cria um "cenário" antes da ação principal acontecer — assim como em português usamos o pretérito imperfeito ("estava caminhando").
