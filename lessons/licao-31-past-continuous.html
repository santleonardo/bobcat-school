📘 Gramática — Past Continuous

Estrutura e uso no dia a dia.

Forma

was/were + verbo-ing

They were playing.

Uso

ação em progresso no passado

I was working at 8.

with Simple Past

contínuo + when + passado simples

I was showering when you called.
