📘 Gramática — Be Going To

Estrutura e uso no dia a dia.

Forma

am/is/are + going to + verbo

She is going to call.

Uso

Planos decididos / previsão com evidência

I'm going to move. It's going to rain.

Pergunta

Are you going to…?

Are you going to come?
