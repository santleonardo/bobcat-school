How to play

  1. Goal: every round shows a word spelled wrong. Figure out which letter is causing the mistake.
  2. Guide the snake (keyboard arrows, swipe your finger on the board, or the ⊕ pad below it) to that letter on the board and eat it — it disappears from the word and the correct spelling shows up right away!
  3. The other letters are traps: eating one of them, or the snake hitting its own body, costs a life ❤️.
  4. 3 lives total. Each correct catch makes the snake bigger and faster. No rush: think before you move!
